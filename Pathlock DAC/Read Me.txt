Kindly follow the steps outlined below to successfully install the Pathlock DAC solution:

Product Installation:
Step 1: Proceed with the software installation by importing the transports provided in Section 2 - 'Pathlock DAC Installation' of the 'Installation Guide' document.
Step 2: Now, implement the Single Point of Interceptions (SPIs) provided in Section 3 - 'SAP GUI (Enhancement / Modification)' of the 'Installation Guide' document to complete the installation. 
Step 3: If the Pathlock DAC team is responsible for implementation, please grant them access to the system, including the necessary roles and authorizations as specified in Section 1.2 of the 'Deployment Guide'. Additionally, ensure the installation is reviewed by the Pathlock DAC team before the workshop session. For assistance, contact the Project Manager.

Additional Information:
1. You can refer to the 'Overview Guide' document to know more about the Pathlock DAC delivered features and it's coverage.
2. To become familiar with the configuration steps please refer the Section 4 - 'Sample Implementation' of the 'Deployment Guide document.

If you encounter any issues or have any questions during the installation process, please do not hesitate to reach out to us for assistance.
